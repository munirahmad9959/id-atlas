# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want id-atlas to be a friendly, harassment-free place for everyone, whatever their age, body size,
disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, level of experience, nationality, appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Welcome behaviour:

- Being kind and patient, especially with people who are new.
- Giving and accepting feedback about the work, not the person.
- Assuming good intent and asking when something is unclear.
- Respecting that people contribute in their own time and in their own language skills.

Not acceptable:

- Harassment, insults, personal attacks, trolling or discriminatory jokes.
- Publishing someone else's private information, such as an address or an email, without permission.
- Posting real identity documents or personal data in any form: this is also against our
  [contributing rules](CONTRIBUTING.md).
- Anything else that would reasonably be seen as inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, issues and pull requests that break this code, and may
temporarily or permanently block a person from the project.

To report a problem, use GitHub's **Report content** option on the comment or issue, or contact the
maintainer (`@munirahmad9959`) through the private channel described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Reports
are handled privately and fairly.

## Scope

This applies to all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews) and to anyone
representing the project in public.
